Frappe Learning is an easy-to-use Learning Management System (LMS) designed to help you create structured courses with diverse content types such as videos, quizzes, and assignments. Whether you are an educator, an organization conducting internal training, or an entrepreneur sharing knowledge, Learning simplifies the process. Most LMS platforms are rigid, complex, and expensive. Learning is different. It is open-source, flexible, and cloud-ready, making it easy to create and manage courses. Why Learning? Because learning should be simple, interactive, and effective. With Learning, you get: Easy Course Creation – Structure your courses with videos, quizzes, assignments, and interactive lessons. Engagement Tools – Solve student queries through discussions and group students into batches. Live Classes – Conduct live sessions by integrating Zoom with the LMS. Assessments & Certifications – Evaluate learners with quizzes, assignments, and certification programs. Automated Scheduling – Manage evaluator slots and send automated invites for evaluation calls. Analytics & Insights – Track key metrics such as signups, enrollments, and completion rates. Career Support – Help students find job opportunities through the Job Board. Key Features Learning will help you: Create and share online courses with your target audience. Structure lessons using text, videos, quizzes, and assignments. Track student progress and engagement through batches. Conduct live classes using Zoom integration. Assess learners with quizzes and assignments. Manage evaluation calls with automated scheduling. Gain insights with real-time analytics on signups and enrollments. Share job opportunities with learners via the Job Board. Who can use Learning? Organizations training employees internally Companies educating users about their products Schools and universities offering online study materials. Entrepreneurs, freelancers, and influencers sharing their knowledge.
