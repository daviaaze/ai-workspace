A Payment Terms Template combines one or more terms into a reusable schedule. Create a Payment Term Go to Home > Accounting > Accounting Masters > Payment Term . Enter a clear name. Choose how the due date is calculated. Enter the credit days or due date basis. Enter an invoice-portion percentage if this term represents one instalment. Optionally configure an early-payment discount. Save. Create a Payment Terms Template Open Payment Terms Template . Add terms in the required order. Enter the invoice portion for each row. Confirm that the total allocation is 100%. Mark it as default only if it should apply broadly. Save. A template can represent Net 30, a deposit plus balance, milestones, or staged payments. Apply payment terms Select the template on a Quotation, Sales Order, Purchase Order, Sales Invoice, or Purchase Invoice. ERPNext creates the payment schedule from the transaction date and the configured rules. Check the schedule before submitting. The invoice schedule drives due dates, overdue calculations, payment allocation, and receivable or payable reporting. Early-payment discounts A Payment Term can define a discount percentage or amount and a validity period. The customer or supplier must pay within that period for the discount to apply. Test the accounting result before making this part of a production collection process. Troubleshooting The due date is unexpected Check the posting date, due-date basis, credit days, and whether the term uses a day of the month. The template cannot be saved Verify that invoice portions total 100% and that every row has a Payment Term. The schedule was not copied Check whether the transaction or party has another template and whether the schedule was edited manually.
