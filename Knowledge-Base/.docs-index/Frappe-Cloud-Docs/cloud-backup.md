Simple restore Automated daily backups Frappe Cloud takes a daily snapshot of your database locally. This feature is available for all plans, starting with our lowest $10/month shared hosting plans. Trigger on-demand and scheduled backups Frappe Cloud also lets you take on-demand backups right before performing critical actions like upgrades, site transfers, etc. This ensures that you have the latest data backed up locally to confidently take action. Automated backup for failover management During upgrades, Frappe Cloud backs up your most recent data. If anything goes wrong, you’re back to where you started with automated fail-safes and recovery, ensuring minimal disruptions to your end-users. Offsite backups for disaster recovery Relying solely on local backups can be risky due to threats such as hardware failures, natural disasters, or cyber-attacks. Offsite backups ensure a copy of your data in a secondary server. They are designed safeguard your data against unforeseen disasters. Offsite backups are available in all $25/mo and above shared hosting plans. Simple restore With Frappe Cloud, you can restore your backups easily. You can restore backups from backup files or directly from an authorised account on Frappe Cloud.
