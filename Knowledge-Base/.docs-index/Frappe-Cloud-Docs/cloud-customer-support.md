Hosting Support 2. Product Warranty 3. Premium Support for Enterprises 4. Other Support Channels Hosting Support Hosting applications on your own can be challenging. You're usually reacting to issues and dependent on developers to debug and fix them. We’ve designed Frappe Cloud to simplify hosting and debugging. However, if you’re stuck during a deployment, make an error during an upgrade, or have billing queries, you can reach out to Frappe support ( support.frappe.io ) and we will help you within defined SLAs . Product Warranty If you encounter an incorrect behaviour or bug in our application, we provide support to fix it. Our support only covers bugs that are replicable in our system. We don’t support bugs that arise due to customisations or 3rd party apps. In such cases, we recommend you to reach out to the respective app developer or partner ecosystem to get help. Currently we provide product warranty for Frappe Framework, ERPNext, Frappe HR, Insights, and Learning. Premium Support for Enterprises For companies that need personalised and priority support for mission-critical and large-scale projects. We suggest premium support for large-scale deployments with enterprise scale and performance requirements. Premium Support includes; A dedicated hosting support agent Priority SLA 24 x 7 incident support On-Call Support Technical consulting for infrastructure and scalability Premium Support is available on premium dedicated hosting and hybrid hosting plans.
