Dedicated resources ensure that the traffic on other sites doesn't affect the performance of your site. With dedicated hosting, you can create unlimited benches and sites. When you opt for dedicated hosting, you get a pair of servers – an application and a database. Representation of multiple servers containing sites and benches What benefits will I get with Dedicated Hosting? With dedicated hosting, you can create unlimited sites and benches for no extra cost. So, go ahead and create any number of benches as separate environments or as isolated containers to deploy your sites. We offer Frappe Product Warranty for any 5 sites that you opt-in under our support plans. Dedicated hosting is easy to scale. If there are days when you need to scale your infra because of the daily compute limit, you can easily do so by opting for a higher plan in just a few clicks. You can configure pager alerts to get notified about resource consumption. This helps you plan and act on exhausting limits proactively rather than reactively. Additionally, you get all the other features to install , upgrade , backup , monitor , develop Frappe applications. What is Enterprise Hosting? When you're using Frappe applications for large or mission-critical projects, you might need more attention from us. Enterprise hosting is designed to offer exclusive support on top of standard dedicated hosting. With Enterprise hosting you get - Priority response time 24x7 incident support Dedicated support agent for hosting On-call support Technical consulting hours Dedicated hosting pricing Always know what you'll pay with flat pricing across all plans.
