Granular access controls Site analytics Monitoring resource utilisation, uptime and debugging performance bottlenecks in self-managed Frappe apps can be challenging. It often leads to a reactive approach rather than a proactive one. As your number of sites grows from tens to hundreds, this complexity can quickly become difficult to manage. Frappe Cloud's site analytics helps you easily monitor CPU, storage, and memory utilisation for all your sites on a simple dashboard. It offers you slow query analytics that help with performance debugging. This never-heard-of transparency enables customers to choose a plan after analysing their needs over time. Server analytics Frappe Cloud's server analytics helps you monitor your server's CPU, storage, and memory utilisation. This helps you upgrade or downgrade the plans as per your needs. Additional metrics include network utilisation and load averages. These help you monitor and improve your server utilisation. Job log Jobs show a history of actions performed on your site such as adding a custom domain, scheduling a backup, updating site configuration and more. You can view this data to check if the jobs are running properly, if something is stuck, or has failed along with reason of failure. Activity log Activity logs provide chronologically organised events on your personal or team account. You get an overview of changes to your environment variables, deployments, and more. Site log Site logs show the dump of log files that are generated by each process such as scheduler.log, frappe.web.log, frappe.log, database.log and web.log. These logs ensure that every activity performed on Frappe Cloud is logged for audit and analysis. Alerts You receive email alerts about important events such as resource limits, administrator login, failed deployments, and more. These alerts enable you to monitor critical events/incidents and take proactive actions. Granular access controls Frappe Cloud is equipped with granular access controls which can be enabled at site, bench, or server level. This is particularly useful if you want to give restricted access to your team members, especially for partners & consultants managing tens and hundreds of customers using Frappe Cloud.
