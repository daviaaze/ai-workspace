comparison how it works Run the site, while Frappe manages the infrastructure Behind the scenes, Frappe rents dedicated servers from cloud providers, splits them into benches, and runs multiple sites on them in a controlled way. Your site shares compute resources with others, but the platform handles scheduling, limits, and stability for you. Compute-based pricing You’re not charged on how long your business operates or how many users log in, but on how much work your site actually performs (such as background jobs, reports, imports, and workflows). This model keeps costs low and removes almost all operational complexity. Read more Made for simple requirements Site plans are designed for a single site and come with limited control over infrastructure. Infrastructure is shared, and performance may be subject to the noisy-neighbour problem. As workloads grow, Server plans are usually a better fit. Ideal for beginners Trials, experimentation, learning, simple production setups, and teams that want the easiest possible starting point. under the hood Private and Shared bench groups Bench groups are a stack of containers to host multiple sites in an isolated environment for specific versions and applications. A new site is by default created under a shared bench group. Shared bench groups simplify hosting tasks, and are ideal for companies looking for out-of-the-box apps with minimal customisations. Hosting your sites on private bench groups creates an isolated environment and unlocks deeper controls. It lets you manage your upgrades, install custom apps, get SSH access, and more. You also get access to 150+ ready-to-use Frappe apps available. Whenever your site needs to take an action, like saving an invoice, loading data, or running a report, it briefly “borrows” some computing power to get that job done. What you pay for You only pay for those tiny moments when your site is actually using the CPU to work, not for every minute you use the site. Some examples include: Saving data Fetching information Running calculations or reports What you don’t pay for If you spend 5 minutes filling out a form, your site might only need a few milliseconds of CPU time when you hit “Save”. You’re charged only for those milliseconds, not the full 5 minutes. How long users use your site How many hours your site is running Idle time when nothing is happening Frequently asked questions What exactly do I get with a Site plan? You get a single site hosted on Frappe Cloud, with the ability to install apps and use standard Frappe Cloud features like upgrades, backups, and monitoring (subject to plan limits). Can I install multiple apps on one site? Yes. You can install multiple apps on a single site, as long as they fit within the limits of your chosen plan. What is Product Warranty? Product Warranty is support from Frappe engineers for fixing bugs in Frappe Apps (e.g. ERPNext, Frappe HR, Frappe Framework) and Frappe Cloud. You can raise tickets from the support portal and our engineers will respond in a stipulated response time. Note that this does not include functional support. Are Site plans suitable for production use? Yes. Site plans are suitable for production workloads as long as the use case is not mission-critical, concurrency of usage is low and occasional variability in performance is tolerable. For more reliable hosting, we recommend Server plans . Can I install multiple apps on one site? Yes, for small or simple production setups. For heavier workloads or multiple sites, Server plans are usually a better fit. Is there a per-user limit? Sites created with any of the plans do not have a user limit. However, these sites are limited by CPU hours per day. This means that if there are a lot of users using the site, the site may slow down if the CPU usage limit is reached. Where are the servers hosted, and where is the data stored? We host most of our servers with Amazon Web Services (AWS EC2), while a few are hosted via OCI (KSA) and Hetzner. You can choose your data centre location across 5 continents and 10 countries. Can I switch (downgrade or upgrade) my plan later? Yes, if you are unsure which shared hosting plan is best suited for you, just start with any plan (e.g., $10/month) and monitor your usage on the Frappe Cloud Dashboard.
